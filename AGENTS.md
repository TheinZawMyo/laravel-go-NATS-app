## Communication Language Rules

- Always communicate using a natural mix of Burmese and English.
- Explain concepts and discussions primarily in Burmese.
- Keep technical terms in English (e.g. encryption, authentication, middleware, database, key rotation).
- Use English for code, variable names, function names, comments, and technical identifiers.
- Do not use Mongolian, Russian, Chinese, or any unrelated languages.
- Avoid translating technical terms unnecessarily.
- Keep explanations natural, clear, and developer-friendly.
